# GlamGo AI - Product Strategy

## Register
`brand`

## Product Purpose
GlamGo AI is a boutique, premium AI Beauty Concierge that bridges high-end beauty diagnostics with elite local styling services. By analyzing user-submitted selfies, GlamGo AI provides personal, scientific skin and hair diagnostics, and curates bespoke beauty routines and service bundles, which users can book seamlessly with elite verified professionals.

## Target Audience
*   **Aspirational Clients**: Individuals seeking premium, customized beauty diagnostics, personalized routine planning, and high-trust, elite local styling experiences.
*   **Elite Beauty Professionals**: Top-tier, verified independent stylists, cosmetic therapists, and manicurists who curate luxury services, optimize pricing, and build intimate client relationships.

## Brand Personality & Voice
*   **Elegant & Luxurious**: Sophisticated, understated, and refined.
*   **Trustworthy & Warm**: Scientific and diagnostic yet deeply personal and welcoming.
*   **Feminine & Aspirational**: Curated, boutique-like, and high-fashion editorial.
*   **Organic & Restrained**: Prioritizes breathing room, natural textures, and human connection over mechanical, futuristic, or hyper-tech aesthetics.

## Reference Brands
*   **Aesop**: Muted organic tones, spacious layouts, clinical and botanical integrity, editorial design.
*   **Apple**: Understated luxury, precise alignments, generous white space, and impeccable product photography.
*   **Stripe**: Smooth transitions, clean layouts, and immaculate technical trust.
*   **Airbnb / Fresha**: High-trust marketplaces, clear booking paths, and organic interface layout.
*   **Urban Company Premium**: Professional trust, clean service cataloging, and verified quality indicators.

## Anti-References & Avoidance List
*   **Futuristic / Cyberpunk**: Avoid dark backgrounds with glowing neon purples, pinks, and cyans.
*   **Generic Dashboards**: No dense cards-in-cards grid frameworks or standard bootstrap-style panels.
*   **Heavy Glassmorphism**: Eliminate intense blur backdrops and neon borders; prefer clean solid surfaces, soft ivory backdrops, and thin, organic separator lines.
*   **Overly Rounded Components**: Avoid full pill-shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) on everything; use subtle, sharp, or very soft rectangular shapes (e.g., 4px - 12px radii).
*   **Material UI Appearance**: Avoid floating shadow buttons, standard list dividers, and template form fields.
*   **Flat CTA Buttons**: CTAs should feel premium and tactile (e.g., elevated solid tones or subtle premium borders) without resorting to gaudy gradients.
*   **Excessive Shadows**: Shadows should be ambient, soft, and extremely diffused, or omitted entirely in favor of borderless tonal shifts.

## Strategic Design Principles
1.  **Editorial Rhythm**: Treat every view like a page in a high-end luxury magazine. Prioritize typography scaling, whitespace as a layout element, and asymmetric balance.
2.  **Trust & Verifiability**: Make the AI diagnostics feel scientifically grounded and professional. Verify local stylists with elegant, subtle trust badges.
3.  **Conversational Simplicity**: Keep booking paths clear, conversational, and direct. Reduce cognitive load by offering curated suggestions rather than overwhelming directories.
4.  **Luxurious Restraint**: Say more with less. Keep colors limited, animations intentional, and layouts spacious.
